# Stakeholder Management

| Stakeholder | Primary Concern | QA Engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Scope, customer value, date | Risk/options/recommendation |
| Engineering | Technical feasibility | Testability, defects, evidence |
| DevOps/SRE | Stability, deployment | Quality gates, observability |
| Security | Risk exposure | Security findings and remediation |
| Business | Revenue/customer impact | Release risk and readiness |
| QA Team | Coverage and execution | Priorities, coaching, blockers |

## Communication Pattern
**Facts → Risk → Options → Recommendation → Decision → Owner → Due Date**

QA communication should be concise, evidence-based and solution-oriented.
