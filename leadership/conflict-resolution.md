# Conflict Resolution

## Example: QA vs Engineering Severity

**Problem:** QA recommends High severity; Engineering recommends Medium.

**Resolution**
1. Reproduce independently.
2. Confirm affected workflows.
3. Quantify customer/business impact.
4. Check frequency and workaround.
5. Review evidence with Product and Engineering.
6. Agree severity and priority.
7. Record rationale.

**Outcome:** The decision is based on impact and evidence rather than hierarchy.
