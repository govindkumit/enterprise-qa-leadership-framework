# QA Team & Capacity Plan

## Team

| Role | Capacity | Primary ownership |
|---|---:|---|
| QA Manager | 1 | Strategy, governance, stakeholders, release |
| QA Lead | 1 | Daily execution, triage, coordination |
| Automation QA | 2 | UI/API automation and CI |
| Functional QA | 2 | Business workflows and exploratory testing |
| Performance/Security QA | 1 shared | Non-functional validation |

## Capacity decision
Release timeline was reduced from 10 to 8 working days. Instead of accepting blanket overtime, the QA Manager:

1. Prioritized P0/P1 business flows.
2. Reused stable regression automation.
3. Moved low-risk exploratory scenarios to post-release validation.
4. Added focused pairing for checkout/payment.
5. Documented residual risk and obtained Product Owner acceptance.

**Leadership principle:** negotiate scope and risk before negotiating unsustainable effort.
