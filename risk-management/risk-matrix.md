# Quality Risk Matrix

| Probability | Impact | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| High | High | Critical |
| High | Medium | High |
| Medium | High | High |
| Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Low | Low | Low |

## Treatment
**Critical:** Block or obtain explicit executive risk acceptance.

**High:** Mitigate before release where practical and track residual risk.

**Medium:** Monitor and test according to business impact.

**Low:** Accept with normal monitoring.
